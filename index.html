516 Beaumont Rd — Broker Price Opinion

516 Beaumont Rd is a 6,456 SF, two-story Class B medical/office building on a 0.36-acre lot, built in 2003, zoned OI with an HSPOD overlay, roughly 0.3 miles from Cape Fear Valley Medical Center. The building is fully vacant — formerly a plastic surgery practice — and currently marketed for lease by Grant-Murray at $19.00/SF/yr NNN. This opinion addresses sale value only. Because the property has no in-place income, the sales comparison approach is primary, led by the two most locationally comparable of seven independently verified Cumberland County closed sales; a hypothetical pro-forma income approach is presented only as a secondary cross-check and is clearly disclosed as hypothetical throughout.

Methodology and verification basis

Because the building is fully vacant with no in-place rent roll, this BPO relies on the sales comparison approach as its primary method. A hypothetical pro-forma income approach is presented afterward strictly as a secondary cross-check, built on an assumed (not actual) market rent, and is labeled as hypothetical wherever it appears — it is never presented as confirmed income.

  • Source of record for comps: the Cumberland County CAMA/tax property system and GIS Tax Parcels layer, which publish deed book/page, buyer, sale price, and building area for every parcel; revenue stamps were cross-checked against every recorded price.
  • Comps were independently researched, not taken from the subject's own lease marketing brochure — every comp below carries its own county-record source link. The brochure's photos and floor plans were used only to describe the subject property, never as comp evidence.
  • Two flagged transactions in the wider comp screening (a same-day quitclaim/warranty deed pair, and stamps that implied a materially different price than recorded) were excluded from the comp set below rather than silently averaged in.
  • The subject's own $19.00/SF/yr NNN lease ask was independently checked against nearby lease comparables rather than accepted at face value — see market context below for the honest disclosure of that comparison.
  • Any hypothetical income figure below assumes a lease-up that has not occurred and a market rent that has not been tested by an actual signed lease at the subject; it is disclosed as a cross-check, not a basis for the recommended list price on its own.

Market context

Cap rates. Fayetteville asking cap rates for office product cluster at 7.6%–8.3%, per LoopNet's Fayetteville office market page (7.63% average) and a local Grant-Murray office listing asking an 8.29% cap. The one local medical asking cap found, 6.50% on a 40,816 SF building 100%-leased to Cape Fear Valley Health, reflects hospital-system credit tenancy that does not apply to this vacant, non-credit asset. Nationally, CBRE's Q2 2026 U.S. MOB Figures put the average medical outpatient building cap rate at 7.1%, while the Boulder Group's Q2 2026 net lease report shows single-tenant net-lease office asking at 7.90%, and CBRE's Class B/C office cap rate brief notes over 70% of such assets nationally still price at double-digit (≥10%) cap rates. Given the subject is vacant, non-credit, small Class B, second-generation medical space, a rate toward the upper end of the local 7.6%–8.5% band is most defensible — materially above the 6.50% credit-tenant local comp, but below the double-digit national Class B/C distress tier given the subject's reasonable condition (30% depreciation) and strong hospital-adjacent location.

Lease rates — honest disclosure on the $19.00/SF NNN ask. Fayetteville's average office asking rent is $21.00/SF and medical-office average is ≈$22/SF per LoopNet and LoopNet medical offices, but those are quoted on mixed lease structures. On an apples-to-apples basis, the two closest true medical comparables near the hospital are $20.00/SF gross and $19.44/SF modified gross — and the only true NNN comp found nearby, a Grant-Murray-marketed medical/office space, is $14.68/SF NNN. Once the NNN structure is accounted for, the subject's own $19.00/SF NNN ask sits at or above the top of the local range, not comfortably within it. Separately, an independent listing aggregator shows this same 6,456 SF space quoted at $24.00/SF/YR — a discrepancy from the brochure's own $19.00 figure worth reconciling with the listing broker, and not relied upon here.

Value analysis

Sales comparison approach (primary). Grant-Murray gives primary weight to 1295 Oliver St and 3416 Melrose Rd, the two comps with the closest locational match to the subject (same VCS 8054 market area and same BORDEAUX subdivision, respectively). Their adjusted $/SF average $200.81/SF. Applied to the subject's county-recorded 6,456 SF, this indicates a value of $1,296,400. The remaining five comps (adjusted average $172.47/SF) provide secondary, supporting evidence for a broader range but carry less weight given greater distance from the subject or differing submarket/asset profile. This primary-comp approach is the basis of this report's opinion of value because the building has no in-place income to capitalize.

Hypothetical income approach (secondary cross-check only). Because the subject is vacant, any income approach must assume a market rent that has not been tested by an actual lease at this property — it is presented here strictly as a cross-check, not a basis for the recommended price. Two rent scenarios were run at 6,456 SF, each with a 5% vacancy/lease-up allowance and capitalized at 8.0%–8.5% (the upper end of the locally supportable range, appropriate for this vacant, non-credit asset):

  • At the brochure's own $19.00/SF NNN ask (flagged above as likely above true market on an apples-to-apples basis): hypothetical stabilized NOI of $116,530.80, capitalized at 8.0%–8.5%, indicates $1,371,000–$1,456,600.
  • At a more conservative, research-supported $16.00/SF NNN (between the $14.68/SF true-NNN comp and the brochure ask): hypothetical stabilized NOI of $98,131.20, capitalized at 8.0%–8.5%, indicates $1,154,000–$1,226,600.

The primary sales-comparison finding of $1,296,400 falls between the two income scenarios — above the conservative $16.00/SF range ($1,154,000–$1,226,600) and below the optimistic $19.00/SF range ($1,371,000–$1,456,600) — bracketing the property's likely value from both a comparable-sales and an income perspective.

Recommended list price: $1,280,000 ($198.27/SF) — set modestly below the $1,296,400 primary sales-comparison indication to reflect that the subject has no exact vintage/size twin in the comp set, carries no sale history of its own (the last money transfer was a 2001 land purchase), and is not sprinklered. This price sits within the range supported by both the sales comparison and the hypothetical income cross-check scenarios.

Reference only: the 2026 assessed value is $988,681 ($153/SF), below this indicated range — expected, since Cumberland County assessed values are known to lag verified market transactions.

Cap rate range supporting this opinion

For a vacant, non-credit, small Class B medical/office building in Fayetteville/Cumberland County, the supportable 2026 cap rate range used in the hypothetical income cross-check above is roughly 8.0% to 8.5% — toward the upper end of the local market band.

Local benchmarks: the Fayetteville office market averages a 7.63% cap rate, with a Grant-Murray office listing asking 8.29% and the market-wide, all-commercial average at 6.88%. The only local medical asking cap found, 6.50% on a fully hospital-leased building, is not applicable here given the subject's vacant, non-credit status.

National benchmarks: CBRE's Q2 2026 U.S. MOB Figures report an average medical outpatient building cap rate of 7.1% (record MOB asking rents, average sale price $307/SF versus $196/SF for traditional office). The Boulder Group's Q2 2026 Net Lease Research Report shows single-tenant net-lease office asking cap rates at 7.90% nationally. CBRE's Class B/C office cap rate brief notes that over 70% of Class B/C office assets nationally still price at double-digit (≥10%) cap rates, while CBRE's H2 2025 U.S. Cap Rate Survey found cap rates broadly steady with most respondents believing they have peaked.

The 8.0%–8.5% range used above sits above the local market average and well above the credit-tenant medical comp, reflecting the subject's vacant, non-credit position, while staying well below the double-digit distress tier reserved for materially older or lower-quality Class B/C stock — appropriate given the subject's reasonable condition and strong hospital-adjacent location.
